# Design review: the apps explain their reasoning instead of answering

**Date:** 2026-11-06. **Scope:** every screen of the iOS app, read in full
(`apps/ios/Fleetwright/*.swift`); Android says the same words and the parity
tests hold it to them, so each finding is both phones. **Asked for:** *"we
had the rule do what the brain would do and it's becoming a brain fuck."*

Nothing here is changed yet. Findings are numbered so a fix can name them.

## The one finding

The apps show their working. A row, a footer or an error does not say what
is true; it says what was checked, from where, what each part answered, why
that was not enough, and what the mechanism underneath is. The person has to
run the rule in their own head to get to the answer the app already has.

The worst single example is still the one that started this, on Add a
hypervisor:

> Nothing at 10.69.10.10 answered as Xen Orchestra, and this phone could not
> reach its Proxmox VE port. rpi-7550 did not look for Proxmox VE: its
> Fleetwright is older than that look. Apply update on its page under
> Machines, then Find it again, or say which it is.

The answer in there is *"Couldn't tell. Pick one."* The rest is the
reasoning, and one footnote (rpi-7550 needs an update).

It is not one screen. The same shape is on the machine page, the Xen
Orchestra setup, the vault, Credentials, the pool page, the console banner,
the session row's Resume reason, and the New session footers.

### How it got this way

It was built on purpose, from three principles in `docs/psychology.md`, and
each is right:

- §"Nothing needs you": the banner is *"the headline, and why it is
  confident"*, because a reassurance with no basis has to be taken on faith.
- §6: say what is wrong **and** what to do about it.
- §7: *"nothing is happening"* and *"I have lost the ability to tell you"*
  must stay distinguishable.

What went wrong is placement. The *why it is confident* was argued for one
line at the top of the Sessions tab. It is now attached to every row, every
footer, every step, every refusal. The evidence that was supposed to back one
banner has become the text of the whole app.

The fix is not to repeal any of the three. It is a rule about **where the
why goes**.

## The rule to adopt

Three levels, and a sentence belongs to exactly one:

| Level | What it says | Length | Example (already in the app) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Row** | The verdict | 1 to 3 words, plus a count or time | `healthy`, `not reporting`, `Waiting for you · 12m`, `2 of 3 linked`, `kept ready · 10.0.0.4 · ends in 2h` |
| **Page** | The result and at most one action | One sentence, two at most | `Not started: the machine needs an update`, `1 session is running on deb132. It will not survive.`, `No word since 10:02. Open Fleetwright to check.` |
| **Why** | The mechanism, the custody, the polling, what was checked and from where | As long as it needs to be, **once per screen**, behind a disclosure or a tap | the Credentials scope list behind "6 scopes" |

Three things stay from the doctrine:

1. **§7 survives, as a word.** *Cannot tell* is still never shown as *none*.
   It is shown as `Unknown` or `Not checked`, not as a sentence naming the
   component that did not answer. Several rewrites below would lose this if
   taken literally (for example turning *"the fleet has no report of this
   machine"* into *"machine not found"*); each fix has to keep the
   distinction.
2. **§6 survives, as a button.** The remedy is still offered. It is a
   button or a link next to the result, not a clause inside the status.
3. **The banner keeps its basis.** `Reassurance.basis` is the one place the
   second sentence was argued for and it stays. Everything else loses it.

And one vocabulary rule: **the codebase's words do not go on screen.** The
app already has person-words for all of them.

| Retire from UI strings | Use | Count today (iOS, UI strings) |
|---|---|---|
| the fleet (as the actor), the coordinator | *your fleet* only as a place; otherwise name the machine or say nothing | 89 / 20 |
| token | *access*, *sign-in*, or nothing | 74 |
| box, boxes, host (for a machine) | *machine* | 20 |
| vault | *saved*, *your credentials* | 20 |
| sealed, seals, minter, relay, carrying | nothing; the custody goes under Why | 19 |
| holds the pool, holder, hold | *manages*, *approved* | 7 |
| the line, a new line, hands over | *the token* in Why only; on the page, *added* | 12 |
| way out, egress | *internet access* | |
| a look, looked, did not look | *checked*, *not checked* | |
| asks to join, lets it in | *joins*, *approve* | |
| pinned (admins), readmit, enrol | *this machine's admins*, *let it back in*, *pair* | |
| cannot tell (shown raw in a row) | `Unknown` | |

Em dashes are not in this review. `CLAUDE.md` keeps them in UI strings as a
decision, and the agents' flags on them are dropped.

## How bad, by screen

**Needs structure, not just words** (the detail has nowhere to go until a
page or disclosure exists):

| Screen | Why |
|---|---|
| Add a hypervisor (Xen Orchestra wizard + policy form), `AddHypervisorView.swift` | 12 footers, 17 texts, the longest 85, 82 and 78 words under controls labelled in 1 to 4 words. Machine rows read like log lines. One router-rebuild warning three times on one form. |
| Hypervisors list + Add, `HypervisorsView.swift` | Reviewed earlier this session; the redesign is in that reply. |
| Machine page, `HostView.swift` | Every section has a footer explaining the subsystem; the reboot grant and its command appear two and three times. |
| Your vault / GitHub on this phone, `RunnersFromPhone.swift` | Prose and buttons with no rows; minter, sealed, boxes, hold. |
| Credentials, `Credentials.swift` | A provider row stacks up to six status lines; every footer explains fan-out. |
| Pool machine joining, `HolderJoin.swift` | Each outcome is a paragraph of custody reasoning. |
| Pool page edge-router card, `Manage.swift` | A column of clause-length lines where a status word per router belongs. |

**Wording only** (the structure is right, the sentences are not): New
session footers, Session page pane footer and Resume reason, VM machine
page, console banners, Proxmox setup (phone and through a machine), Deploy
XO, Watchdog, Set up Claude, Linked repositories, Machines' silent-machine
reasons, notification bodies.

**Already right** (the models to copy): the Sessions header
(`stateSentence`), the Reassurance banner, every Siri reply, the
notification buttons, every confirmation and done message on the pool page,
the Machines rows, Files, XO setup step words and end headlines, the
Credentials `describeCheck` line with scopes behind a disclosure.

## Findings

Format: location · what it says now (trimmed) · what it should say. The
rewrite is a direction, not final copy. Pattern letters: **A** mechanism
narrated · **B** reasoning in place of a verdict · **C** sentence where a
row wants a word · **D** instruction inside a status · **E** codebase
vocabulary · **F** the same fact twice on one screen · **G** footer longer
than the control it explains.

change, the parity test, and the doc, opened together.

   vocabulary table go in `docs/design-system.md` beside the dials. A test in
   the style of `test/design-tokens.test.js` fails a UI string in the two
   apps that uses a retired word (minter, sealed, way out, holds the pool,
   the line, box). The existing `*-in-apps` tests keep the phones equal; this
   one keeps them in person-words. Numbers above: the vocabulary table.
2. **Hypervisors list + Add.** Already designed. 59.
3. **Xen Orchestra wizard and policy form.** The biggest, and the one most
   people will meet first after 2. 60 to 90, 115 to 117.
4. **Machine page.** 15 to 27.
5. **Vault, Credentials, Set up Claude, joining.** 118 to 159.
6. **Sessions, New session, Session page, console, VM machine page.** 1 to
   14, 28 to 55.
7. **Pool page, edge card, the through-a-machine screens.** 91 to 114.

   them to fit.

words; every page says the result and offers at most one action as a
control; the mechanism is under one disclosure per screen; `Unknown` is
still never shown as *none*; no retired word on screen.
